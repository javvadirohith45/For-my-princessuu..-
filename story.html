Chapter 01 · Where it all began

From Friends to Love

From a simple friendship to something I never imagined I would call my forever.

I still love the fact that something so simple became something so important to me.

Simple friendship ila naa life lo antha important ayipoyindani gurthucheskunte, ippatiki naa heart full aipothundi. 🥹❤️

🤝

First… Friends

Nuvvu naa life loki first oka friend ga vachav. Appudu mana friendship ila oka beautiful journey ga maaruthundi ani nenu assalu imagine cheyyaledu.

Everything beautiful had to begin somewhere. Ours began with friendship.
🫂

Then… Best Friends

Mana conversations, care, understanding, silly moments — everything became a little more special. Slow ga naa every little thing share cheskovalani anipinche person nuvvu ayyav.

💗

Then… Besties

Friendship kanna ekkuva, oka home la feel avvadam start ayyindi. Somewhere between all those little moments, something beautiful happened.

❤️

And Then… Love

Aa besties madhya eppudo, teliyakundaane oka beautiful love puttindi. Manam plan cheyyakundaane, love mana madhya beautiful ga puttindi.

Mana old memories anni gurthucheskunte… first conversations, silly fights, arguments, laughs, care, understanding — anni okkasari naa mind lo run avuthunnayi. 🥺❤️
