My Trust in Us

Because love is not only what we say. It is what we protect, respect and choose every day.

Trust, for me, is not a big promise. It is the quiet feeling that I can believe in you and feel safe with you.

Naaku trust ante pedda words kaadhu nanna — nee meedha naa heart ki unna calm feeling. Nuvvu genuine ga untav ani namme aa feeling. 🤍

🤍

Care & Loyalty

Nuvvu chupinche care, concern, loyalty naaku chaala deep ga feel avuthayi. Your care is something I feel more than I hear.

Past & Growth

Past kanna ippudu manam okariki okaram ela untunnam, entha genuine ga love chesthunnam anedhe important. Growth ni, efforts ni nenu value chestha.

🫂

Trust In My Heart

Mana trust ni iddaram kalisi jagrathaga chusukovali nanna. I want our trust to feel safe, mutual and protected.

Nenu ninnu just love cheyyadam kaadu… naa whole heart tho ninnu nammutunna. 🥹❤️
